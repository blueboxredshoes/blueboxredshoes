a party went wandering is an upcoming ARG and browser-based game designed to be played by a live audience.

Players are invited to a party that blurs the boundaries between physical/virtual space. Here, they are invited to take turns as the outsider, where they are drawn away from the crowd in order to play a HTML computer game that sits at the front of the room. This game (or “elaborate alternate world”), narratively centred around a virtual party running in parallel to the ‘real’ one, urges players to make collective decisions and negotiate with each other in the physical space in the ultimate endeavour to ‘break the ice’ between strangers.

The game interrogates what it means to belong and be part of something, by pushing players to the outside (of the room, the game, the self).
